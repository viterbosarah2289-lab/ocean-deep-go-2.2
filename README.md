## 🌊 Game Overview

**Ocean Deep GO** is an immersive multi-level ocean adventure game featuring four unique gameplay mechanics: Memory, Racing, Chase, and Survival challenges. Explore the depths, remember creatures, race to the finish, and survive impossible odds!

### Quick Start
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Choose a level from the main menu
3. Follow the on-screen instructions
4. Complete all 4 levels to win!

## 🎮 Game Levels

### Level 1: TANI - Memory Challenge ⭐
- **Objective**: Remember 5 different fish and their names
- **Gameplay**: 
  - Meet the 5 fish: Clownfish, Blue Tang, Angelfish, Butterflyfish, Lionfish
  - Cover your eyes and memorize them
  - Recall and identify the fish
  - Bonus round: Spot the new fish among familiar ones
- **Duration**: 3 rounds
- **Scoring**: Points based on accuracy and memory retention

### Level 2: NOAM - Turtle Adventure Racing Game 🐢
- **Objective**: Race your chosen turtle to the finish line
- **Gameplay**:
  - Select your turtle: Shelly, Turbo, Coral, or Wave
  - Roll the dice to move across the board
  - Land on special spaces for bonuses/penalties
  - **Space Types**:
    - 🏆 Treasure: Move forward 2 spaces (+250 points)
    - ⚪ Safe: Nothing happens
    - 🚫 Obstacle: Move back 2 spaces (-50 points)
    - ❓ Surprise: Collect bonus points
- **Duration**: 3 rounds to reach FINISH
- **Scoring**: Points for reaching finish line and collecting treasures

### Level 3: ELYAS - Chase & Survival Game 🦑
- **Objective**: Save the fish from the hungry squid!
- **Gameplay**:
  - 4 fish start at fixed positions
  - Giant squid hunts them down at increasing speeds
  - Click on fish to save them before the squid catches them
  - Fish scatter when squid attacks
  - Lose a life if the squid catches a fish
- **Duration**: 5 rounds of increasing speed
  - Round 1: SLOW
  - Round 2: A BIT FASTER
  - Round 3: FAST
  - Round 4: VERY FAST
  - Round 5: EXTREME SPEED
- **Scoring**: 100 points per fish saved × round number
- **Win**: Save all fish for 5 complete rounds

### Level 4: DHEER - Nuclear Survival Challenge 💣
- **Objective**: Survive the awakened BLOOP and its nuclear bomb
- **Gameplay**:
  - Dangerous creature surfaces and releases a nuclear bomb
  - Find shelters to hide from radiation and shockwaves
  - Collect power-ups: Shield, Speed Boost, Extra Life, Health Pack
  - Avoid dangers: Nuclear bomb, shockwaves, debris field, radiation zones
  - Manage health and survive 60 seconds per round
- **Duration**: 5 rounds of increasing difficulty
  - Round 1: WARM UP - Easy
  - Round 2: THE HUNT BEGINS - Harder
  - Round 3: NO ESCAPE - Insane
  - Round 4: TOTAL CHAOS - Extreme
  - Round 5: FINAL DOOM - Impossible
- **Win Condition**: Survive all 5 rounds without losing all lives

## 📊 Scoring System

```
Level 1 (TANI):
  - Base: (correct/total) × 100 × (round × 1.5)
  - Bonus: Extra points for bonus round
  
Level 2 (NOAM):
  - Finish line: 1000 points
  - Treasure spaces: +250 points each
  - Obstacle penalty: -50 points
  
Level 3 (ELYAS):
  - Per fish saved: 100 × round number
  - Example: 100 × 1 = 100 (Round 1), 100 × 5 = 500 (Round 5)
  
Level 4 (DHEER):
  - Survival bonus: Points for each round survived
  - Power-up collection: Varies by type
```

## 🚀 How to Play

### Starting the Game
1. Clone or download the repository
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser
3. You'll see the main menu with 4 level options

### Level 1 (TANI) - Memory
1. Study the 5 fish carefully for 20 seconds
2. Cover your eyes (eyes emoji appears)
3. Type the fish names you remember
4. Check your answers - get points for accuracy
5. Complete bonus round to earn extra points

### Level 2 (NOAM) - Racing
1. Choose your turtle (Shelly, Turbo, Coral, or Wave)
2. Click "ROLL DICE" to move
3. Navigate the board following space rules
4. First to reach FINISH wins the round
5. Win 3 rounds to complete the level

### Level 3 (ELYAS) - Chase
1. Study the 4 fish positions
2. Click on fish to save them from the squid
3. Fish move positions each round
4. Squid gets faster each round
5. Save all fish for 5 rounds to win

### Level 4 (DHEER) - Survival
1. Read about the nuclear threat
2. Navigate the survival area
3. Collect power-ups (Shield, Speed, Health)
4. Avoid dangers (bomb, shockwaves, radiation)
5. Survive 60 seconds per round
6. Complete all 5 rounds to beat the impossible
